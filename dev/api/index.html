API

MeromorphicLoci.AAA — Type
AAA(; nsamples = 320, tol = 1e-13, max_degree = 150, stagnation = 5)

Zeros and poles of f(z) from a rational fit to its samples. A box zdomain is sampled evenly at about nsamples points, and confirming a candidate near its edge may evaluate f slightly outside; an array zdomain is the samples, with zeros and poles reported wherever the fit finds them.

  • nsamples — the fit finds at most about half as many zeros and poles as it has samples, and fewer in practice: 60–100 have needed 1000–4000. Too few miss some silently, with fitconverged = true. Use more for features narrower than the spacing.
  • tol — the fit stops once it matches the samples to tol relative to their largest magnitude; a zero or pole whose effect on them is smaller goes unfound. Noise in f above tol stalls the fit (fitconverged = false); zeros and poles buried in it are lost.
  • max_degree, stagnation — the fit stops at this degree, or after this many steps without improving.

Samples where f is NaN are skipped: return NaN where f cannot be trusted. unresolved lists candidates where f is too noisy or undefined to confirm them.

MeromorphicLoci.ArgumentPrinciple — Type
ArgumentPrinciple(; zres, minsep, zoom = 4)

Zeros and poles of f(z) in the box zdomain = (zlo, zhi) by adaptive refinement of cells where the phase of f winds.

  • zres — resolution of the points before the polish, and the scale at which zeros and poles are told apart: those closer than about zres fuse into one of their net winding, e.g. two simple zeros read as one double zero.
  • minsep — the closest zero–pole spacing the search must see: a closer pair can cancel and vanish, unflagged by deficit. Zeros (or poles) closer than minsep are still found, at extra cost. Sets the cost floor, about 2·area/minsep² evaluations.
  • zoom — how many times, at most 8, to halve below zres to separate a zero and a pole of equal order; a pair closer than zres/2^zoom is reported unresolved. Of unequal orders, they stay fused as under zres.

Besides the common fields, returns deficit: the box's count of zeros minus poles, less the windings found (of both kinds, as findzeropoles reports them). Nonzero means some were missed, often beside a found zero or pole of high order: divide known factors out of f. nothing means the winding around the box can't be read: f is non-finite on its edge, or a zero or pole sits there (enlarge the box slightly).

MeromorphicLoci.Branch — Type
Branch{M} <: AbstractVector{Sample{M}}

One locus z*(p) followed over M parameters, as Samples sorted by parameter.

  • b.winding: the multiplicity; 0 for a lone sample whose kind could not be read.
  • b.degree: how many loci the branch joins at one parameter point (Several parameters); 1 for a single-valued z*(p).
  • b.uncertified: stretches (p1, p2) where another locus came within zfloor, so the branch may have switched to it, and (p, p) where following stopped at p (What is certified).

MeromorphicLoci.Continuation — Type
Continuation(seed = AAA(); zres = nothing, zfloor = nothing, certify = true)

Loci over parameters for findloci: the point finder seed finds them at the knots, and muller follows them between. How it works and what it can miss.

  • zres — the scale of a step's correction. nothing takes seed.zres of an ArgumentPrinciple seed, and a quarter of the sample spacing of an AAA seed.
  • zfloor — loci closer than this are not told apart (uncertified of a Branch); two closer throughout are one locus. At most zres; nothing takes zres/256.
  • certify — count the zeros minus poles in the box at each knot against the branches (deficit of the Survey). Always on for an ArgumentPrinciple seed.

MeromorphicLoci.Survey — Type
Survey{M} <: AbstractVector{Branch{M}}

The Branches over M parameters. s.deficit lists p => n at the knots where $n = N(p) - \sum_b w_b$, the zeros minus poles in the box less the windings of the branches there, is nonzero (The count), and p => nothing where $N(p)$ cannot be read. $n > 0$ means the seed missed loci at p. s.nevals counts the evaluations of f(p...)(z).

MeromorphicLoci.findloci — Function
findloci(f, zdomain, pdomain, alg = AAA(); mapper = map) :: Survey

Loci z*(p) of the zeros and poles of f(p...)(z) in the box zdomain = (zlo, zhi) as p ranges over pdomain.

pdomain holds one entry per parameter: an interval (lo, hi), searched at equispaced knots (8 for one parameter, 6 each for more), or a sorted vector of knots.

MeromorphicLoci.findzeros — Function
findzeros(f, zdomain, alg = AAA(); polish = Muller(), mapper = map)

Zeros of f(z) in the box zdomain = (zlo, zhi) of opposite corners. Returns a named tuple:

  • points: sorted by real, then imaginary part.
  • windings: multiplicity, negative for a pole. Points closer than alg resolves merge into one of their summed winding.
  • converged: whether polish (Muller) met its tolerance. Only points of winding ±1 are polished; polish = nothing keeps the estimates of alg.
  • unresolved: zeros or poles detected but not classified.
  • nevals: evaluations of f.
  • fitconverged (AAA) or deficit (ArgumentPrinciple).

Zeros and poles where f overflows to Inf or underflows to 0 go unfound: divide out exponential growth, or shrink the box.

MeromorphicLoci.muller — Method
muller(f, x0; h, radius = Inf, tol = 1e-10, maxiter = 100)
muller(f, x0, x1, x2; tol = 1e-10, maxiter = 100)

Muller's method for a zero of f, from a guess x0 uncertain by about h or from three starting points. Returns (; root, converged, nevals): root is the smallest-|f| point evaluated (NaN if none was finite), a zero when converged, i.e. a step fell below tol·max(|root|, h) (h the starting points' spread). Steps stay within radius of x0; keep h and radius below the distance to any other zero, or it may converge there.

Where noise or rounding in f limits accuracy above tol, as at a multiple zero, root lands about that limit away, typically unconverged.
